# Contributing to Awesome-Construction-Accounting

We welcome contributions from the community! Whether you are a developer, an accountant, or a construction tech enthusiast, your insights help keep this list valuable.

## How to Contribute

1. **Fork the Repository**: Create your own copy of the repository.
2. **Add Your Entry**:
   - Locate the appropriate category (SaaS or Open Source).
   - Add the project/product with a clear, factual description.
   - Use keywords that help others find the tool (e.g., "WIP reporting", "AIA billing").
3. **Submit a Pull Request**: Provide a brief summary of what you added and why it fits the "AI-native" or "Agentic" theme.

## Criteria for Inclusion

- **AI-Native/Agentic**: The tool should use AI for more than just simple OCR. It should involve reasoning, automation, or predictive analytics.
- **Construction Specific**: Tools must be designed for or highly adaptable to construction workflows (job costing, lien waivers, etc.).
- **Active & Maintained**: We prefer links to active products or repositories.

## SEO/GEO Optimization

When writing descriptions, try to include specific industry terms like:
- Job Costing
- WIP (Work in Progress) Reporting
- Lien Waiver Management
- AIA Billing Automation
- 3-Way Invoice Matching
- Cash Flow Forecasting for Contractors

Thank you for contributing!
